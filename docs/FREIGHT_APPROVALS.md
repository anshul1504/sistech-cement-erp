# Consolidated freight review and retained claim evidence

SRS §3.2 and FR-LOG-013/014: transporter bills retain contracted freight, capped allowed extras and shortage recovery, with independent review before becoming payable.

Freight bill detail, My Work and the approval-routing catalog now support the same independent review eligibility. A site-only direct checker must cover every trip in the consolidated bill. Named central routing and dated delegation require explicit existing company-finance and freight-bill read scope. A company-scoped grant covers a consolidated bill, rather than pretending that one site grant covers a multi-site claim. Both principal and deputy must remain enabled/unexpired and qualified. Grant creation does not add roles, read access, sites or finance scope.

The bill maker cannot decide directly, be routed as an independent reviewer or lend approval authority through a deputy. The actual deputy remains `checked_by`; retained delegation use references `freight:{bill_id}`. Contract caps, detention/unloading caps, shortage recovery, advance deductions and variance findings remain unchanged. Approval/rejection web writes now require recent identity confirmation as well as CSRF and ordinary access checks.

Approval posts once through the existing source-keyed balanced ledger. Failed variance validation or posting rolls back the decision, its snapshot and delegated use. A repeated decision cannot post/consume authority twice. Routing is accountable and nonexclusive: it does not itself decide, grant permission or post money. A qualified configured backup can receive a disabled reviewer's pending assignment; stage completion removes it from pending inboxes while retaining routing/history.

## Rejected-claim history

A decision now freezes the bill calculation and line-level trip identity/site, vehicle/customer display, contract/claim amounts, extra/recovery amounts, detention and reason. Rejection releases the live one-to-one trip links for a corrected bill, while retaining the original claim snapshot. Later corrected bills do not overwrite it. The rejected detail renders the retained line evidence. Approval likewise retains its calculation evidence independently of future rate/trip changes.

Rejected bills retain existing visibility rules: their creator and explicit central finance may view them; removing live trip links does not grant arbitrary site users access to the retained customer/claim data. Earlier rejected bills whose lines were already deleted have no trustworthy historical line data. Their snapshot stays empty and the screen states that evidence is unavailable; no backfill invents a past review.

Migration `erp.0047` adds the review snapshot field. A SQLite backup was saved outside Git before application. Counts for all 131 existing non-metadata business/account tables remained unchanged; no live review snapshot, approval, grant, staff assignment or ledger posting was created. Existing rows start with empty snapshots rather than fabricated decision evidence. Applied-migration and migration-drift checks passed.

## Freight entry validation and authoritative trips

Entry now validates money before comparisons/arithmetic: claims, extras and advance must be finite non-negative decimal values within their declared digit/paise capacity. Detention days must be whole, non-negative and fit the positive-small-integer column. Required bill date/reference and reference length are checked before insertion. Header totals are validated too; individually valid lines whose aggregate exceeds the header capacity roll back the entire new bill/line set. Advances above the calculated amount retain the existing zero-payable floor; this change does not invent a new payment policy.

The service takes the transporter lock before checking duplicate references, then locks selected dispatches in primary-key order. Passed trip instances supply IDs only: persisted site, transporter, state, payer, frozen contract and lane are authoritative. Existing site access is checked against that persisted site. Already billed, wrong-transporter or no-longer-billable trips fail without creating a claim. The one-to-one trip constraint remains a second safeguard; PostgreSQL concurrent verification is still a rollout gate.

Bill references are checked case-insensitively across existing claims, including rejected ones, matching the existing non-reusable vendor-reference identity. Rejected claim evidence is never overwritten; a corrected claim uses a distinct reference. Exact rejected-reference reuse now returns a validation error rather than reaching the database uniqueness error. Entry does not grant read, site or financial scope or change approval controls.

No schema migration, live bill edit, claim, staff grant, financial posting or hardware call is needed. Broader freight reconciliation, late POD/correction lifecycle, PostgreSQL races and company acceptance remain separate scope.

## Pending approval versus late POD evidence

A pending bill can be entered before POD. Review now compares current persisted trip state, company payer/transporter, frozen contract, allowed extra cap, current shortage/damage recovery and retained header calculations before approval. A shortage cannot silently become zero recovery because its linked invoice quantity basis is missing. If financial evidence differs, approval fails without changing the claim, consuming delegated authority or posting a journal. The reviewer can reject the pending claim and the maker can enter a corrected bill with the current recovery. The original rejected claim and source evidence remain retained.

The web form carries a fingerprint of the current bill/trip/POD/invoice basis. Missing/stale fingerprints fail before decision; a POD with no recovery change can be approved after refreshing and reviewing its current evidence, without unnecessary claim reentry. The bill screen explains mismatches and hides **Approve payable** while leaving qualified independent rejection available. A forged approval POST is still rejected by the service. Trusted internal service calls may omit the display fingerprint but cannot bypass the financial/source mismatch checks; future APIs must pass current reviewed evidence too.

Decision holds the bill and ordered trip rows while comparing and posting, so concurrent POD writes cannot change the checked trip during approval. Transporter entry serialization uses a non-key-update mutex, preserving compatibility with normal foreign-key references; PostgreSQL race/deadlock/load validation remains a rollout gate. The review snapshot now retains the observed source basis and digest alongside the original line calculation. It includes invoice identity/quantity/taxable basis and POD status/quantities/receiver/evidence text, without duplicating bank data or uploaded file bytes. Validated amounts are normalized to paise before fingerprinting.

No schema migration, live approval, rejection, delivery, routing or financial posting was performed for this milestone. Existing approved/rejected snapshots are not rewritten. **Post-approval** late-POD reconciliation/recovery adjustments remain separate pending scope; this control protects pending bill decisions and does not claim to complete that later lifecycle or the whole SRS.

## Post-approval late-POD recovery and paid credits

An approved bill remains an immutable original claim. New shortage/damage recovery can be requested from its detail page using the current POD/invoice evidence fingerprint, dated no earlier than the original bill/latest local POD date and no later than today. The request retains the observed source basis, previous approved reductions, cumulative recovery and resulting effective payable. Exact retries return the same request; changed retries fail. Rejected/cancelled requests remain retained and a fresh request may follow. One pending request per bill is enforced in the database.

The requester and original bill maker cannot review. Existing company-finance freight approval authority supports direct or dated delegated review, actual actor receipts, assigned inbox/handoff and My Work. Recent web identity confirmation applies. Approval rechecks current evidence, open period and payment reservations under transporter/bill/ordered-trip locks. Rejection retains evidence without posting. Failure rolls back both the decision and delegated-use receipt. Original bill amounts, lines, approval and paid allocations remain unchanged.

A positive approved reduction posts **Dr transporter payable / Cr freight expense** through the existing GL engine. Effective payable subtracts approved reductions. Already-paid reductions create a vendor credit: for example, ₹9,000 paid followed by ₹1,600 recovery leaves a −₹1,600 open freight item. Selecting that credit against another ₹9,000 bill stores signed allocations of −₹1,600 and +₹9,000, resulting in a ₹7,400 net bank payment. Mixed supplier/freight liability offsets retain balanced ledger signs. Current and cutoff-date subledger controls include the approved recovery on its effective date; close checks flag missing recovery journals.

Existing proposed/approved allocations block recovery approval until explicitly cancelled/reviewed. Positive open bills with unreconciled POD changes are held. Payment approval/release locks its freight sources and rechecks current evidence, total reservations and backing of reserved credit. A returned original payment can invalidate an unspent reserved credit; its release is then blocked for explicit reallocation. Payment returns share the transporter mutex. Bank release remains an internal record requiring a supplied bank reference; this implementation performs no external transfer.

The original zero-payable floor is retained. A further increase in recovery at that floor can be independently acknowledged with zero liability change and no empty journal. Decreases of previously recognized recovery, liability restoration, cash refunds, pure zero-cash settlement and damages beyond the original liability floor require separate workflows and remain pending. SQLite tests and browser checks do not certify PostgreSQL locking/deadlock behavior, bank delivery or company finance acceptance.
